## Moving Bin Rental Agreement

This Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is between **Beehive Bin Co.** of Clinton,
Utah ("we," "us") and the customer signing below ("you"). By signing, you agree
to the terms below for the rental of reusable moving bins and lids
(the "Bins").

### 1. What you're renting

- Package: **[10 / 20 / 40 / 60] Bins** with matching lids, delivered on
  **[START DATE]** and picked up on **[RETURN DATE]**.
- The Bins remain our property at all times. They are inspected and cleaned
  between rentals.

### 2. Rental period and fees

- Rentals run **one week** from your delivery date. First-week rates: 10 bins
  $39 · 20 bins $79 · 40 bins $129 · 60 bins $179, plus Utah sales tax.
  Delivery and pickup within our Weber & Davis County service area are
  included.
- Extra weeks, by request and subject to availability: +$25 (10 bins), +$40
  (20), +$65 (40), +$90 (60) per week, plus tax.
- Deliveries and pickups happen in the evening; we'll confirm an exact window
  with you. **We do not deliver or pick up on Sundays.**

### 3. What we require

- A valid government-issued photo ID, presented by the person signing this
  Agreement.
- A valid credit or debit card kept on file with us (through Square) for the
  duration of the rental.
- **No security deposit is collected.**

### 4. Card on file authorization

You authorize us to securely store your payment card with our payment
processor, Square, and to charge that card, without further signature, for:

- the rental fees and any extra weeks you request;
- late returns, at the applicable extra-week rate for each week or partial
  week the Bins are kept past the agreed return date without an approved
  extension;
- missing or unreturned bins at **[$__ per bin]** and missing lids at
  **[$__ per lid]**;
- damage beyond normal wear, up to the replacement rates above per damaged
  item.

Normal wear from ordinary moving use is expected and never charged. We will
email you a receipt for every charge. This authorization ends when the Bins
are returned and any amounts owed are settled.

### 5. Care and use

- Use the Bins for ordinary household packing and moving. Don't store
  hazardous materials, liquids likely to spill, perishables left long-term, or
  anything illegal in them.
- Keep the Bins reasonably clean and dry, and indoors or under cover overnight.
- Don't mark, sticker, or write on the Bins.

### 6. Non-return

If the Bins are not returned or made available for pickup within
**[14] days** of your return date and we cannot reach you, the full
replacement rates in Section 4 will be charged for all outstanding items, and
we may treat the Bins as unreturned property.

### 7. Cancellation and rescheduling

Cancel or reschedule free of charge up to **[24 hours / 2 days]** before your
delivery date by phone, text, or email. **[Decide: any fee for later
cancellations?]**

### 8. Your belongings

The Bins are provided as-is for packing and transport. We are not responsible
for damage to items you pack in or move using the Bins, and you agree not to
hold us liable for such damage except where caused by our own negligence.

### 9. General

This Agreement is governed by Utah law. If any provision is found
unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. It is the entire agreement between
us about this rental and replaces the website summary where they differ.
