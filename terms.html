Rental Terms

The full rental agreement is presented and accepted before every rental. The highlights:

Submitting the reservation form is a request, not a booking. Nothing is reserved or charged until we confirm with you directly and you sign the rental agreement.

Rentals run one week from your delivery date. Extra weeks are +$25 / +$40 / +$65 / +$90 by package, by request and subject to availability. We do not deliver or pick up on Sundays. You don't need to be home for delivery or pickup — bins are left at and collected from your front door, and bins set out for pickup remain in your care until we collect them.

A valid government-issued photo ID and a credit or debit card must remain on file for the duration of the rental. No security deposit is collected.

Your card may be charged, as authorized in the rental agreement, for missing, unreturned, or damaged bins at $15 per bin (lid included — bins and lids are replaced as a set), late returns (including bins not set out for a scheduled pickup), or approved extensions. Normal wear from ordinary moving use is expected.

Bins remain the property of Beehive Bin Co. and are inspected and cleaned between rentals.

Cancel 48 hours or more before your delivery date for a full refund; inside 48 hours, we refund 50%. Rescheduling is free anytime, subject to availability.

Prices and availability shown on this site are subject to confirmation and may change. Site content and branding belong to Beehive Bin Co., and these terms and every rental are governed by Utah law.
