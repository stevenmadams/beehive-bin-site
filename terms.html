Rental Terms

The full rental agreement is presented and accepted before every rental. The highlights:

Rentals run one week from your delivery date. Extra weeks are +$25 / +$40 / +$65 / +$90 by package, by request and subject to availability. We do not deliver or pick up on Sundays.

A valid government-issued photo ID and a credit or debit card must remain on file for the duration of the rental. No security deposit is collected.

Your card may be charged, as authorized in the rental agreement, for missing or unreturned bins, missing lids, damage beyond normal wear, late returns, or approved extensions. Normal wear from ordinary moving use is expected.

Bins remain the property of Beehive Bin Co. and are inspected and cleaned between rentals.
