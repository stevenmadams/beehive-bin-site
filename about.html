About us

A Local Alternative to Cardboard

Beehive Bin Co. is a locally operated reusable moving-bin rental service based in Clinton, Utah. We started with a pretty simple question:

Why are people still spending money on piles of cardboard boxes every time they move?

Heavy-duty plastic totes are easier to pack, easier to stack, don’t require tape, and don’t need to be thrown away afterward. So we built a simple rental service specifically for Weber and Davis Counties.

  • No giant service territory
  • No complicated packages
  • Just affordable moving bins when you need them

Why Rent Instead of Buy Cardboard?

Quick answers

The Three Things Everyone Asks

Is there a security deposit?

No.

We require a valid card on file, photo ID, and acceptance of our rental agreement instead.

Do you deliver?

Yes — home delivery and pickup are included in every package within our service area.

Prefer not to share your address? We're happy to meet at a public location instead.

How long do I get the bins?

Rentals run one week at a time, and you can add extra weeks whenever you need them.
