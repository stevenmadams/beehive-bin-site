No Security Deposit

We don’t want to hold hundreds of dollars of your money while you’re already dealing with moving expenses. There is no security deposit. Instead, we require:

  • Valid government-issued photo ID
  • Valid credit or debit card on file
  • Acceptance of our rental agreement

Your card is only charged beyond your original rental when permitted under the rental agreement, such as for missing bins, significant damage, or bins that are not returned on time.
